## Promotional image

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a polished text-free small promotional image for the Chrome extension Image Extractor, landscape aspect ratio 11:7, intended final size 440x280. Input image is a brand reference: preserve its recognizable blue photo-frame, white interior, blue mountains and yellow sun symbol in a prominent icon on the left. On the right arrange a compact grid of small image cards flowing into one larger photo card, communicating browsing page images and inspecting one image larger. Cards show tasteful mountain landscapes and botanical details. Clean modern graphic design, saturated royal blue backdrop with subtle tonal depth, white card surfaces, restrained yellow accents consistent with reference, generous breathing room. Fill canvas edge to edge. Clear silhouette at thumbnail scale. No text, letters, numbers, watermark, Chrome or Google logos. Not an actual UI screenshot. Generate at 1320x840 or equivalent exact 11:7 composition.
